Concept D — The Key Map. Every bound atlas opens with a key map: the whole territory at small scale, with each plate outlined on it. That is exactly what front matter is for, and it is what the left leaf should be — not three lines of text. Hover a milestone, a dot in the Effort Field, a territory tile or a record crown and watch the country answer. The camera never moves: the tiles are fetched once, at one zoom, and never again — all motion belongs to the ink, which is map.js's own founding law.
