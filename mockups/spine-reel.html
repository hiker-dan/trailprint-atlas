THE REEL. Time-proportional spacing is gone: every hike is one bead of equal presence, and the chain slides beneath a fixed brass needle — no dead winters, no crushed trip weeks. Trips ride as brass-bound strips that unspool when the needle enters them. Drag or scroll the reel to scrub; the land re-inks bead by bead. Press ▸ to ride.
