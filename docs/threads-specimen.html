Design Specimen — July 2026

Threads of the Trail

Three registers for the homepage milestone narrative, each built from the same seven real milestones. Hover any station for its detail card. The line icons are placeholders in a consistent hand-drawn style — every slot can take commissioned art later.

Register AThe Serpentine Waypath

The trail switchbacks down the page the way a path descends a slope. Compact: seven milestones in about a third of the height a straight timeline needs. Stations sit at the bends; cards tuck into the curve.

Register BThe Field Sheet

A surveyor's sheet: parchment, faint contours, a compass rose. Milestones are stations plotted on the map, connected by a dotted route that reads left to right, then down — the most "real map" of the three.

Register CThe Ridgeline Ascent

The whole journey as one climb: a ridgeline rising left to right, milestones planted on the slope, the line fading onward past the last station — the story isn't finished. The most space-efficient: one wide band.

Same seven milestones everywhere: dates, trails, and photos are real Atlas data (hand-copied for this specimen).
