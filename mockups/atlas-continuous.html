Mockup — The Continuous Expedition (Concept 5). There is no hike "page": the whole Atlas is one map, and clicking any trail — or any dot on the spine — raises the light-table sheet over the land. The camera cuts behind the veil to frame the trail in the strip the sheet leaves open; the land out there stays alive — scrub the sheet's acetate and the marker walks the real trail behind it. LOWER THE SHEET (or Esc) and you're exactly where you were: same camera, same spine, nothing reloaded. The spine up top never blinks through any of it — that persistence IS the concept. URL carries ?sheet=tta_NN so every risen sheet is still a shareable link.
