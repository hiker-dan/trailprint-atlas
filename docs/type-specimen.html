Scout Lookout via West Rim Trail

Zion National Park • Springdale, UT
Hiked on June 26, 2025

Field Notes

A steep, paved climb from the canyon floor into cool Refrigerator Canyon, then up Walter's Wiggles, a set of twenty-one tight switchbacks engineered into the cliff, to the sandy perch of Scout Lookout. From here the knife-edge spine of Angels Landing rises just beyond, and the West Rim and Zion Canyon fall away in every direction.

Flora SpotlightBigtooth maple (Acer grandidentatum) — the tree that sets Refrigerator Canyon ablaze each fall, thriving in the cool shade where the sun rarely reaches.
