Trail Echoes

The Local Loop

Runyon Canyon · the hometown trail · 2022–2025

I lived down the street from Runyon for years. I was brand new to LA and had barely any money, and hiking Runyon multiple times a week was a great free way to get out. The views at the top were always showstoppers. I remember countless days making it to the top of the fire road, to a clearing on the hill with benches, where I would always take a seat to catch my breath and stare out at the city.

As I grew into my love for hiking, weekly trips up Runyon were replaced by the weekly trip to Angeles National Forest. I couldn't find a single time I hiked Runyon in 2024, as I was eager to explore bigger, newer, or more isolated trails.

I moved to Silver Lake in 2025. A couple of days before I moved, I hiked it one last time as a trip down memory lane. A last hurrah.
