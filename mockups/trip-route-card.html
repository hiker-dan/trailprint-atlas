Concept 5 — The Route Card. The land is the page. Hovering or clicking an itinerary row cuts the camera to that stop behind a soft veil (never a flight — the Atlas rule) and raises a field slip. "Whole trip" at the top of the itinerary returns to the full expedition view. Every row is a door into its field log.
