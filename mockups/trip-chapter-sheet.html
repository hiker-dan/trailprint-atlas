Concept 1 — The Chapter Sheet. The page opens on the map's own chapter card, which dissolves into the sheet. The dashed Expedition Line runs the whole page as its spine; the journey map and every stop are stations planted on it. Stops are launchers: name, numbers, one photograph, a door. The star plate up top is mounted like a photographic print, not stretched like a web banner.
